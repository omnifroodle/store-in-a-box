IN A BOX

The store that divides and merges without stopping.

Pack a store by scanning it into a box. Sell at a venue with no signal. Hand eight jackets to a tablet that walks away and keeps selling. Merge back in any order. Plug in, and every unit and every dollar is accounted for. Agents planned the trip; the tablets ran the day.

See why it never conflicts Get the code

Every node is a store.

The chain, the box, a tablet, a phone. Same documents, same gestures, different sizes. Two of them that meet reconcile with each other and are done.

Custody, not counts

A unit is wherever it was last scanned.

Inventory is modelled as units in custody, not numbers to be merged. Scan a unit onto the box and the store's allocation hands it over. Scan it onto a tablet and the box hands it over. Sell it, and the tablet hands it to a customer with a tender attached. Pack, split, merge and sell are one gesture and one document shape.

1 query proves the whole trip: the sum over every custodian never changes.1
  • Nothing to decrement. A sale is one more movement, written once. Counts are worked out from the movements, never stored and edited.
  • Scans outrank records. Check in a unit the record says is elsewhere and you get an exception, not an error.
How custody works in Couchbase Lite →

Conflict-free by construction

Two sales of the last unit become one exception. No node ever has two versions to choose between.

Every movement of a unit is a document written once and never edited, and each one names the movement before it. Counts are worked out from the movements, so there is no shared number for two tablets to fight over. When the flagship and a tablet both sell the last rain shell, the ledger holds two movements where there should be one: a fork in that jacket's history. Every tablet and HQ run the same rules over the same movements, find the same fork, and file it as one exception with both sales attached. A person decides.

2 branches kept, 0 winners picked. Disputed, not lost and not sold twice, until HQ chooses.2
  • Immutable movements. Pack, take, return and sale are each one document, written once by one device.
  • Each names its predecessor. prev_txn makes a unit's history a chain; two movements that follow the same one are a fork.
  • The same reducer everywhere. Tablets and HQ run the same rules, held to the same golden fixtures. The box just carries the documents.

Device-to-device sync

Split the store. Merge in any order.

Tablets replicate to each other directly. Three at one booth are three replicas of the same store with no primary. Walk one out of range with eight jackets on it and it keeps selling. Split three of those onto a phone. Bring the phone back to the tablet and the tablet back to the box, or the other way round; the answer is the same.

0 coordinators. Two custodians that meet reconcile with each other and are done.3

The cloud finds out later and has nothing to adjudicate.

How splitting and merging work →

Pull the cable

Nothing on this side of the room noticed.

The box is a Couchbase Edge Server in a backpack. It holds the whole packed dataset, serves the tablets, runs the small model for the clerk, and phones home when it can. Cut its uplink and the venue carries on. Power it off and the tablets keep each other current. Throttle it to 2G and only the cloud's view gets slower.

KB not MB. A day of sales reconciles in kilobytes; a 10,000-SKU price change arrives as a few hundred.4

Every write is local and correct the moment it lands. Replication is continuous and opportunistic and never blocks the thing that produced the data. The cloud is informed, not consulted.

How the box phones home →

Hybrid search on the tablet

Goes with this. Makes sense here.

Scan the second item and the tablet proposes an add-on. Vector similarity to the basket, fused with a full-text match on tags and structured filters for category, price band and the weather, in one SQL++ statement on the device. A custody join means it only ever offers what is in the box.

<1 s on the tablet, radio off. The small model on the box adds the reason; kill the box and the suggestions still come.5
-- vector half, on the tablet
SELECT META(p).id, p.name,
       APPROX_VECTOR_DISTANCE(p.embedding, $basket) AS d
FROM store.product p
WHERE p.category_family IN $complements
  AND p.price BETWEEN $lo AND $hi
ORDER BY d LIMIT 20;
-- keyword half: MATCH(p.tags, "waterproof socks pack-cover"), same filters
-- fused by rank, then joined to this custodian's allocations

How the upsell advisor works →

Offline loyalty

Credit travels like inventory.

A member's offline allowance is reserved in Capella, allocated to the box and split to tablets, then enforced on the device with no lookup. The QR is signed with a key the tablet already holds; the second factor is four digits checked against a salted hash. Pessimistic by policy, encrypted at rest, and still a real sale.

$0 of card risk. It is the retailer's own credit to its own customers, capped by a number on a policy document.6

Spend past the allowance at a second tablet and it declines, offline, and says why. Two slices overspent across tablets become an exception with both sales attached, never an overdraft.

How offline loyalty works →

Compliance agent

Every claim carries its passage.

Before the trip, an agent finds what the venue requires: licence, tax registration, tent inspection, organiser permit. Each claim is retrieved with the jurisdiction as a hard filter, drafted with the passage it came from, ground-checked by a second model, and tested for freshness and jurisdiction match by code that is not a model at all.

3 checks, three states. Verified, needs confirmation, not found. Never a bare percentage.7

We poison one source before the demo. Watch the agent catch it. Then the clerk calls the county from the parking lot, and that phone call becomes a source for next time.

Capella AI Data Plane · possible, not in this demo

Agent Catalog could version every tool and prompt the agent uses, so each claim names the exact version that made it. A cache in front of compliance questions would key on the jurisdiction, so Richmond, Virginia never answers for Richmond, California.13

How the agent proves its work →

Permit flow

The agent drafts. The office vets. The field confirms.

Each permit is one document with a state machine. The agent writes it as drafted and can go no further. The back office vets, files, and attaches the approval. The box carries it to the venue. The clerk shows it to the inspector, records the visit with a photo, or flags that the booth moved into a different fire zone. All offline. The office sees it when the box phones home.

2 directions. Approvals flow down; inspections, confirmations and flags flow up.8

Policy gates what the box may do per state: no account tender until tax registration is approved. Flip it from HQ; the tablet honours it after the next sync and names the permit that caused it.

How the permit flow works →

Sync with Capella App Services

Nine lines decide what reaches the venue.

A sync function validates each document, checks the writer holds the trip, and routes it to the trip's channel. The box receives its trip, its region's catalog without cost or margin, its region's members, and nothing else. The other forty thousand SKUs are not hidden on the tablet. They were never sent.

0 silent losses. Two sales of the last unit become one exception with both sides attached.9

Custody never reaches App Services as a conflict: every sale is its own document, and forks are found by the same ledger on every node. Lose access to a channel and the documents leave the device on their own. No admin credential anywhere.

How channels and sync work →

Capella

Once it syncs, it is just data. Then it is the next trip.

Data a tablet wrote in a field with no signal is ordinary Capella data when it lands: SQL++ for the conservation query and the exception queue, Columnar for the venue scorer, Search for the compliance agent's retrieval, Eventing for reorders, AI Services and Agent Catalog for the agents. The explainer writes the end-of-day narrative. The retrospective is evidence for the scorer's next run.

5 agents before the trip, three on the box, two after. None calls another. They read and write documents.10

Why this approach, and why now

The store stopped being a place. The database followed.

Retail went to where the customers are

Pop-ups, markets, festivals, drops, trunk shows, stadium concessions. The venue changes weekly and the connectivity is whatever the organiser's router does. A POS that needs the cloud is a POS that needs luck.

Sync moved to the device

Couchbase Lite replicates peer to peer. A tablet is not a client of anything; it is a replica that can meet another replica. That is what makes a split store a store and not an outage.

Custody beats counts

Count-based inventory has one failure mode and every offline system eventually finds it. Units in custody conserve, and the proof is one query that works while two custodians are out of range.

Agents are only as good as their evidence

An agent that files permits is a liability. An agent that drafts them with the passage attached, gets caught when a source is poisoned, and learns from the clerk's phone call is a colleague.

ON THE TABLET

Every scan, sale, split and merge. Hybrid search for the upsell. Loyalty verification. The permit and the policy. Encrypted, conserved, peer to peer.

ON THE BOX

The whole trip's dataset. The small model for the clerk and the briefing. No custody logic: it carries the documents, and the tablets and HQ run the ledger. The link to the cloud, used when it exists.

IN CAPELLA

The system of record. Channels and the sync function. The agents that plan, the agent that explains, and the eval harness that keeps them honest.

Tech specs

What's inside.

On the device
Couchbase Lite with the Vector Search extension and database encryption. Scopes store, agents, ref; same names as the cluster.
Device-to-device
Couchbase Lite peer-to-peer replication between tablets and phones, and to the box. No server in the path.
The box
Couchbase Edge Server on a macOS laptop in Phase 0, or a Raspberry Pi where Edge Server supports its OS, plus a small model runtime for the on-box agents.11
Sync
Capella App Services over a WebSocket. Channels per trip, region and box. One sync function, nine lines. Custody never conflicts: movements are immutable and the ledger finds forks on every node.
Cloud
Capella: Data Service, Search (hybrid and geo), Columnar, Eventing, AI Services, Agent Catalog.
Custody
Allocation tree with parents; immutable movements, each naming its predecessor; counts derived, never stored. Conservation checked live. Scan to check out, scan to check in; unit-level QR for the demo.
Upsell
Hybrid SQL++ on the tablet: APPROX_VECTOR_DISTANCE plus full-text MATCH, rank fusion, custody join. Weight is a policy value.
Loyalty
Signed QR (retailer key, validity window), salted phone hash, per-member allowance reserved in Capella and split like inventory.
Compliance
Hybrid retrieval with jurisdiction filter, second-model ground check, deterministic freshness and jurisdiction checks, 20-site gold set, zero false verifieds to ship.
Seed data
300 to 500 outdoor-apparel SKUs with embeddings, two stores, 18 months of synthetic sales, three past pop-ups, three to four real jurisdictions' ordinance text.
Room to grow
The Capella AI Data Plane could add hosted models with semantic caching and guardrails, AI Functions in SQL++, vectorization workflows, and Agent Catalog for every agent's prompts as well as its tools. Possibilities, not part of this demo.12
Not in scope
Real payments, PCI, receipt printing, filing permits, social media ingestion beyond an optional labelled feed, fleet optimisation.
Status
Spec complete. Build in phases; Phase 0 is sync and custody with no AI. See the spec for acceptance criteria.
